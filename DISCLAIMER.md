# Disclaimer

AndoFrontier Lab is a forensic video analysis toolkit for technical review.

It does not prove the origin, nature, intent, or identity of any object in a video. Results are method-dependent and must be interpreted with source quality, compression, sensor behavior, tracking validity, controls, and uncertainty in mind.

No original videos are included in this public repository. Users are responsible for lawful handling of any media they analyze locally.

Generated reports and Reddit templates are drafts for review, not publication guarantees. Public statements should avoid origin claims and should clearly distinguish observation, measurement, inference, and speculation.
